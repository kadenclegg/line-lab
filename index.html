How signals work

Strong signal means the model differs from the market by at least 5 points on the spread or 4 on the total. Lean means at least 3 on the spread or 2.5 on the total. Coin flip means the numbers are close. Experimental variants are tracked separately from the main model.

Model signals

Every game where the model meaningfully disagrees with the market — the watchlist we're grading over time.

Model picks

Biggest model-vs-market disagreements, ranked as calibration candidates. These are model leans, not betting picks — the model has never beaten the closing line.

How picks are ranked

Overs and Unders rank games where the model’s total differs from the market by at least 2.5 points. Spreads rank games where the model’s spread differs by at least 3 points. Same thresholds as the Lean signal.
