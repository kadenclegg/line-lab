How paper markers work

Strong means the model differs from the market by at least 5 points on the spread or 4 on the total. Lean means at least 3 on the spread or 2.5 on the total. Coin flip means the numbers are close. Test lines are active experiments tracked separately from the production model.

Paper signals

Every game where the model meaningfully disagrees with the market — the watchlist we're grading over time.

Model picks

Biggest model-vs-market disagreements, ranked as calibration candidates. These are model leans, not betting picks — the model has never beaten the closing line.

How picks are ranked

Overs and Unders rank games where our total differs from the market by at least 2.5 points. Spreads rank games where our spread differs by at least 3 points. Same thresholds as Paper lean markers.
